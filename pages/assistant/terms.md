# Eric Personal Assistant — use and limitations

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This is a private assistant for Eric Norman's personal use. It is not a public subscription service and does not accept public sign-ups. This information page does not grant anyone access to the assistant or its connected accounts.

The assistant helps organize email follow-ups and calendar preparation. Its summaries, classifications, proposed dates, and other outputs can be incomplete or incorrect. They should be checked against the source messages and calendar before relying on them. The assistant is not an emergency service, and notification delivery or availability is not guaranteed.

The current Google integration is read-only. Sending messages, changing calendars, making purchases, and entering commitments require separate applicable authorization from the account owner. Connecting an account does not grant these additional powers.

The account owner may stop using the assistant or revoke its Google access at any time. See the [privacy policy](/assistant/privacy/) for processing, retention, and deletion information.
